## Descrição (rascunho)
The premise is made up. The physics is real: why the sun's light and gravity take 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach us, what happens to the Moon and the planets, how plants and plankton depend on sunlight, why ponds freeze first, and why the deep ocean could stay liquid for a very long time.
