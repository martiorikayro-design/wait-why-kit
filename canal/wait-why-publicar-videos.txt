TITLE:
The Theft That Made the Mona Lisa Famous

DESCRIPTION:
In August 1911, a handyman walked out of the Louvre with the Mona Lisa wrapped in his work smock. Nobody noticed for a whole day. The theft turned her into the most famous painting in the world.

Sources:

TITLE:
Why Birds Can Eat the Hottest Chilies

DESCRIPTION:
Chili "burn" isn't real heat. It's capsaicin tricking the pain sensor that warns you about burns. Birds have that sensor too, but capsaicin can't trick theirs. That helps the pepper: mice chew its seeds to bits, while birds swallow them whole and spread them far away.

Sources:
Tewksbury et al., PNAS (2008): https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0802691105
Jordt & Julius, Cell (2002): https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(02)00637-2
University of Florida (Tewksbury & Nabhan, Nature 2001): https://archive.news.ufl.edu/articles/2001/07/for-peppers-hot-quite-literally-the-spice-of-life-uf-research-shows.html

#shorts #science #animals #chili #funfacts

(Tradução: O "ardor" da pimenta não é calor de verdade. É a capsaicina enganando o sensor de dor que avisa sobre queimaduras. Os pássaros têm esse sensor, mas a capsaicina não engana o deles. Isso ajuda a pimenta: ratos mastigam as sementes, e os pássaros engolem inteiras e espalham longe.)

TITLE:
Animals That Live the Longest (#1 Might Never Die)

DESCRIPTION:
Ranking the longest-living animals, from 6 to 1. Our detective gets a little older with every entry.

6. Jonathan, a giant tortoise: about 194 years (Guinness World Records)
5. A bowhead whale: about 211 years (estimated)
4. A Greenland shark: at least 272 years
3. Ming the ocean quahog clam: 507 years
2. A deep-sea glass sponge: about 11,000 years
1. The immortal jellyfish (Turritopsis dohrnii): when it's hurt or old, it can turn back into its polyp ("baby") stage and start over.
