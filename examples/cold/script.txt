The coldest place in the universe is colder than space itself. Here are the coldest things, ranked. Number six: Antarctica, minus one hundred twenty-nine degrees, the coldest air ever measured. Five: liquid nitrogen, minus three hundred twenty-one. Four: Pluto, minus three hundred ninety-three. Three: craters on the Moon that never see sunlight, as low as minus three hundred ninety-seven. Two: the Boomerang Nebula, minus four hundred fifty-eight. And number one: absolute zero, minus four hundred sixty. Nothing can ever be colder.
