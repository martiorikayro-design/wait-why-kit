The Mona Lisa became a superstar because someone stole it. In August nineteen eleven, a handyman slipped into the Louvre in a white work smock. He'd helped put the Mona Lisa behind glass. He lifted her off the wall, wrapped her in his smock, and walked out. Nobody noticed for a whole day. Guards figured she was off being photographed. When the museum reopened, crowds lined up to stare at four empty hooks. She spent two years in a trunk in his apartment. He didn't steal the world's most famous painting. He made it.
